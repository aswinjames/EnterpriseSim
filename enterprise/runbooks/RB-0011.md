## Purpose

Give the on-call Incident Commander (IC) a single, ordered playbook for running any MCG
incident from declaration to resolution and postmortem hand-off. This is the generic
process runbook referenced by every service runbook's Escalation section. It codifies the
roles, cadence, comms and the rollback-vs-fix-forward decision so that any engineer pulled
into IC duty — regional squad on-call for a Tier-1/2 issue, or SRE (TEAM-032) for a Tier-0
Sev1 — runs the incident the same way. INC-2026-007 and INC-2026-018 (both Sev1, promo-cache
staleness co-owned by TEAM-004/-007/-008) are the canonical examples of multi-team, multi-
region incidents this checklist is built for.

## When to use (symptoms / alerts)

- Any PagerDuty page that a responder cannot resolve inside the first acknowledgement window,
  or any alert with customer-visible impact on a Tier-0 app (APP-001, APP-002, APP-003,
  APP-004, APP-005, APP-006, APP-007, APP-009, APP-010, APP-012, APP-014, APP-019, APP-024).
- A responder invokes a service runbook and that runbook's Escalation step points here.
- Two or more squads are simultaneously paged for what looks like one root cause.
- Any event where you are unsure of severity — declare first, downgrade later.

## Preconditions

- You have `mcg-incident` CLI access and are in the SRE on-call PagerDuty schedule, or you
  are the owning squad's regional on-call acting as IC for a Sev3/Sev4.
- Slack and the `mcg-incident` bot are reachable; StatusPage publisher role available for
  customer comms (Sev1/Sev2 only).
- You know MCG severity definitions (CANON §6): **Sev1** revenue/safety-critical outage,
  **Sev2** business-critical degradation, **Sev3** important but contained, **Sev4** minor.

## Procedure

1. **Declare the incident and set severity.** Open the incident record; the ID is minted as
   `INC-<YYYY>-<###>`:
   ```
   mcg-incident declare --title "<short symptom>" --sev <1|2|3|4> \
     --apps APP-### --regions <NA|EU|APAC|LATAM,...>
   ```
   Severity rubric: customer transactions failing or revenue stopped = Sev1; degraded/slow or
   partial = Sev2; contained with a workaround = Sev3; cosmetic/internal = Sev4. When in
   doubt, declare one level higher and downgrade.
2. **Open the incident channel.** The bot auto-creates `#inc-<yyyy>-<nnn>`; if not:
   ```
   mcg-incident channel open INC-2026-###
   ```
   Pin the incident record, the affected dashboards, and the runbook(s) in play. This channel
   is the single source of truth — all decisions land here, not in DMs.
3. **Assign roles.** State them explicitly in the channel so there is no ambiguity:
   ```
   mcg-incident role assign --ic @you --comms @lead --ops @lead --scribe @lead
   ```
   - **IC** — owns the incident, makes the calls, does not touch keyboards on the fix.
   - **Comms lead** — internal stakeholder + customer-facing updates (StatusPage).
   - **Ops lead** — drives the technical mitigation with the owning squad.
   - **Scribe** — timestamps every action/decision into the incident timeline.
   For a small Sev3/Sev4 the IC may also scribe; never merge IC and Ops lead on a Sev1.
4. **Establish severity-based update cadence.** Post the first status within 5 minutes:
   ```
   mcg-incident update INC-2026-### --status investigating \
     --note "Impact: <what/where>. Next update in <N>m."
   ```
   Cadence: **Sev1 every 15 min**, **Sev2 every 30 min**, **Sev3 hourly**, Sev4 at
   resolution. Post updates even when there is no change ("still investigating, next update
   HH:MM") so stakeholders never chase you.
5. **Follow-the-sun IC hand-off (Tier 0).** For any Tier-0 Sev1/Sev2 that will cross a
   regional boundary, hand IC at the shift edge NA → EU → APAC (CANON §2.3):
   ```
   mcg-incident handoff INC-2026-### --to @eu-sre-oncall --brief "<state, mitigations, open threads>"
   ```
   The incoming IC re-states ownership in-channel and confirms the current mitigation and
   open decisions before the outgoing IC drops. SRE (TEAM-032) is IC of record for Tier-0
   Sev1; owning squads keep Ops-lead duties.
6. **Customer comms (Sev1/Sev2).** Comms lead posts to StatusPage; keep it factual, no
   internal jargon, no root cause until confirmed:
   ```
   mcg-statuspage post --incident INC-2026-### --state identified \
     --component "Checkout" --message "Some customers may see errors at checkout. We are working on it."
   ```
   Update StatusPage on the same cadence as the internal channel.
7. **Decide: rollback vs fix-forward.** Make the call explicitly and record it:
   - **Roll back** when the trigger is a recent release/config change and a rollback is
     safe and fast (expand-contract migrations mean schema never blocks rollback, CANON
     §7.4). Default for Sev1 with a clear culprit release.
   - **Fix forward** when rollback is riskier than the bug (e.g., would reintroduce a worse
     regression), when the change is already fully rolled out and depended upon, or when a
     one-line flag flip mitigates faster than a redeploy (see RB-0018).
   - Respect the dependency graph: never roll a downstream service (wallet APP-013) ahead of
     its upstream (payments APP-012) ahead of checkout (APP-003).
   Record the decision: `mcg-incident decision INC-2026-### --note "Rolling back REL-2026-### per owning squad; ETA 10m."`
8. **Drive mitigation to done.** Hand the technical steps to the Ops lead / owning squad
   runbook. IC tracks the clock, unblocks resources, and pulls in dependent teams (e.g.,
   TEAM-030 for Kafka/edge, TEAM-060 for the data platform) as needed.
9. **Declare resolved.** Only after impact metrics are nominal and Verification in the
   owning runbook passes:
   ```
   mcg-incident resolve INC-2026-### --note "Impact ended HH:MM; metrics nominal 15m; monitoring."
   ```
   Downgrade before resolving if impact ended but you are still monitoring.
10. **Schedule the postmortem.** Every Sev1/Sev2 requires a blameless postmortem; assign an
    owner and a `KN-###` doc within 24h (Sev1) / 72h (Sev2):
    ```
    mcg-incident postmortem schedule INC-2026-### --owner @squad-lead --doc KN-###
    ```
    The postmortem feeds Reflection/Experience (CANON §9) and any runbook or pre-release-check
    improvements.

## Verification

- Incident record has IC, comms lead, ops lead and scribe assigned and a complete timeline.
- Impact metric for the affected app(s) is back within SLO for at least 15 continuous minutes.
- StatusPage shows `resolved` for Sev1/Sev2; internal channel has a closing summary.
- A postmortem `KN-###` is scheduled with a named owner and due date.
- `mcg-incident show INC-2026-###` reports `status=resolved` with `resolved_at` set.

## Rollback

- This is a process runbook; there is nothing to roll back. If a mitigation you directed
  (rollback, flag flip, restart) made things worse, revert that specific action, note it in
  the timeline, and re-evaluate the rollback-vs-fix-forward decision at step 7.
- If you declared the wrong severity, correct it (`mcg-incident update --sev`) rather than
  closing and re-declaring — the incident ID is immutable and must be preserved.

## Escalation (follow-the-sun, Tier 0)

- **T0:** Owning squad regional on-call is IC for Sev3/Sev4 and initial Tier-1/2 response.
- **T1 (Sev1, or Sev2 unresolved at 20 min):** SRE (TEAM-032) assumes Incident Commander;
  owning squad(s) retain Ops-lead duties.
- **Follow-the-sun (Tier 0):** rotate IC at the shift boundary NA → EU → APAC per CANON §2.3;
  the incident channel is the single source of truth across hand-offs.
- **Executive / major-incident:** Sev1 > 60 min or revenue-critical pulls the SRE duty
  manager and the owning domain's EM into the channel for exec comms.
