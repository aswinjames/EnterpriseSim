# ADR-0047: Experience Decay via Down-Weighting, Not Deletion

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-16
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-03, ARCH-05, ADR-0005, ADR-0036, ADR-0046

## Context

Experience (EXP-###) is append-only (ADR-0005) and accumulates forever (ARCH-03). But not every
lesson stays true: a service is re-architected and a warning no longer applies, or newer
evidence contradicts an old conclusion. ARCH-03 names the **stale lesson** failure — an
experience that no longer holds continuing to be applied — and its lifecycle includes a
**Downweighted** state that reduces relevance while keeping history. The temptation is to delete
a wrong lesson, but deletion destroys the learning history, breaks lineage (executions,
evaluations and reflections reference it), and forfeits the ability to audit or reverse a
learning decision — the core safety property that learning is data, additive and reversible
(ARCH-05).

## Decision

We will handle experience decay by **down-weighting, never deleting**, per ARCH-03:

- When newer evidence contradicts a stored experience, its applicability confidence and
  retrieval weight are reduced (Downweighted); it is not removed. Contradiction rate and outcome
  lift drive the weighting (ARCH-03 confidence model).
- A down-weighted experience remains retrievable and can **recover** if new corroborating
  evidence arrives — the lifecycle explicitly allows Downweighted → Retrievable (ARCH-03).
- Full lineage is preserved: every `EXP-###` keeps its links to origin execution, evaluation and
  reflection (ADR-0005), so the learning history stays auditable end to end.
- Recency and non-contradiction are inputs to retrieval ranking, so fast-moving areas naturally
  surface newer lessons while stable areas retain older ones; principled per-domain decay models
  are a planned refinement (ARCH-03 future evolution).

## Consequences

- Positive: the learning record is never destroyed — decisions are auditable and reversible, and
  a prematurely-discarded lesson can come back when it becomes relevant again; complements
  Knowledge freshness (ADR-0046) on the earned-memory side.
- Positive: resists both stale-lesson application (down-weighting) and hasty forgetting
  (recovery path), giving the Experience Layer a self-correcting quality.
- Negative: monotonic accumulation grows storage without bound; retrieval must respect weights
  efficiently, and lesson sprawl (near-duplicate low-value lessons) must be countered by
  consolidation and value-based ranking (ARCH-03).
- Negative: a down-weighted-but-wrong lesson still occupies the corpus and could, with weak
  contradiction signals, resurface; weighting must be tuned against measured outcome lift.
- Neutral: down-weighting is distinct from promotion (ADR-0036) — a contradiction can also block
  or reverse a pending promotion, keeping bad lessons out of Knowledge.
