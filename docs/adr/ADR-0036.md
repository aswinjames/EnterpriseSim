# ADR-0036: Governed Promotion of Experience to Knowledge

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-03-31
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-03, ARCH-02, ADR-0004, ADR-0007, ADR-0047, ADR-0046

## Context

The ECL keeps two kinds of durable memory. **Knowledge** (KN-###) is governed truth, authored
deliberately, immutable once published (ADR-0004), and the only source of truth (ADR-0007).
**Experience** (EXP-###) is *earned* — the sediment of execution, evaluation and reflection,
append-only, and sometimes wrong or narrow. The flywheel's final stage (CANON-001 §9.8) is
promoting validated experience back into Knowledge, raising the baseline for every future task.

The danger is obvious and named in both ARCH-02 and ARCH-03: **promotion without proof** — a
lucky one-off or an over-generalized lesson becoming enterprise "truth." Once something is
Knowledge it is cited as authoritative and is immutable, so a bad promotion is expensive to
unwind. Promotion cannot be automatic.

## Decision

We will require **governed promotion** for any experience to become Knowledge:

- Experiences start narrow and low-confidence and gain confidence only through repeated
  corroboration and measured outcome lift in the Experience Layer (ARCH-03). Only experiences
  crossing a value + corroboration bar are *proposed* for promotion by the Learning Engine.
- Promotion is a governance action into the Knowledge Layer (ARCH-02), not a write the Learning
  Engine performs unilaterally; it produces a new immutable `KnowledgeObject` (ADR-0004) with
  full provenance back to the originating executions, evaluations and reflections (ADR-0020).
- The promoted experience remains in the Experience Layer for traceability even after its lesson
  is folded into Knowledge (ARCH-03 lifecycle); promotion prunes duplicate lessons.
- Promoted Knowledge is subject to freshness SLAs (ADR-0046); experiences that are contradicted
  are down-weighted, not deleted (ADR-0047), and a contradiction can block or reverse a pending
  promotion.

## Consequences

- Positive: prevents one-off luck and over-generalization from becoming durable truth;
  preserves the integrity of Knowledge as the single source of truth (ADR-0007).
- Positive: full lineage from Knowledge back to the executions that earned it — auditable and,
  because learning is data not weights, reversible.
- Negative: promotion lag — a genuinely good lesson waits for corroboration before it becomes
  authoritative; the organization relies on Experience retrieval in the interim.
- Negative: governance overhead (human-in-the-loop review for high-stakes lessons, ARCH-05
  future evolution) adds latency and effort.
- Neutral: the corroboration bar is a tunable policy; too high starves Knowledge, too low risks
  bad promotions — its calibration is itself a subject for Learning Engine measurement.
