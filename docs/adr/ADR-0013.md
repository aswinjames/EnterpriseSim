# ADR-0013: Confidence Is Composable and Honest

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-16
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-07, ARCH-01, ARCH-02, ARCH-03, ARCH-04, ARCH-06, ADR-0014, ADR-0017

## Context

Several layers form judgments under uncertainty: the Context Layer estimates coverage
sufficiency, the Knowledge Layer scores authority and freshness, the Experience Layer scores
applicability, and the Evaluation Layer expresses judgment certainty. If each layer keeps its
uncertainty private and hands upward only a clean-looking answer, Decision Intelligence
(`ARCH-06`) makes control decisions on laundered certainty — it cannot tell a confident result
from a lucky guess. This is the most dangerous failure class for an autonomous system: acting
decisively on a foundation that was actually shaky.

We must decide how uncertainty flows through the architecture.

## Decision

We will treat confidence as a **first-class, composable, and honest** property that flows
upward. Every layer that forms a judgment must *emit* its confidence as an explicit signal
(coverage confidence, knowledge authority, experience applicability, evaluation judgment
confidence). Decision Intelligence **fuses** these into an overall decision confidence that
drives control (`ADR-0014`). Two rules make it honest:

- **No layer launders uncertainty as certainty.** A weak match, a stale fact, or a
  low-confidence model-assisted judgment must be reported as such, never rounded up.
- **Distinct kinds of confidence are kept distinct** — notably outcome confidence versus
  judgment confidence in Evaluation (`ADR-0017`) — so fusion combines like with like.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** Uncertainty becomes actionable: DI can re-retrieve, replan, or escalate
  *because* a layer said "I'm not sure," rather than discovering the shakiness only after a bad
  action. This is the architecture's primary safety property (`ARCH-07`).
- **Positive.** Composability means a single weak input can appropriately pull down the fused
  confidence, preventing "confidence collapse" where one layer's doubt is ignored by others.
- **Negative.** Every layer now carries the burden of *calibrating* and reporting confidence
  honestly; a miscalibrated layer (over- or under-confident) degrades fusion. Calibration must
  itself be measured against outcomes over time.
- **Neutral.** Confidence is a signal, not a verdict; how it is fused and what thresholds gate
  action are separate decisions (`ADR-0014`), allowing the fusion policy to evolve without
  changing what layers emit.
