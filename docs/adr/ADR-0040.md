# ADR-0040: Human-in-the-Loop on Low Confidence / High Risk

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-04-28
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-06, ADR-0014, ADR-0013, ADR-0017, ADR-0041

## Context

Enterprise AI Workers act on real MCG artifacts — they open PRs against Tier 0 services, touch
PCI and auth code, and run tests that gate releases. Autonomy at the wrong moment is dangerous.
ARCH-06 establishes confidence-gated autonomy as the ECL's safety backbone: Decision
Intelligence fuses the confidences reported by every layer (context coverage, knowledge
authority, experience applicability, evaluation judgment — ADR-0013, ADR-0017) into an overall
decision confidence and never takes high-stakes autonomous action at low confidence.
**Overconfident autonomy** — acting on a bad plan without escalating — is the first failure mode
DI must prevent.

## Decision

We will require **human-in-the-loop** whenever fused decision confidence is low or the change is
high risk, extending ADR-0014 (autonomy gated on confidence):

- DI maps fused confidence to control: **high** → proceed autonomously; **medium** →
  re-retrieve/replan to raise confidence before acting; **low** → escalate to a human or abort
  with a clear explanation (ARCH-06).
- Risk-adaptive thresholds tighten for high-risk changes regardless of confidence — Tier 0/PCI
  and auth changes (CANON-001 §4, §7) require human review/approval, and relax for low-risk
  internal work. A high-stakes change at low confidence is always escalated, never forced.
- Escalation is a first-class outcome with a rationale, not a failure: the Worker hands off with
  its decision trace (ADR-0021), the assembled context, and its uncertainty made explicit
  (confidence-honest, never laundering uncertainty as certainty).
- Escalation rate and its reasons are signals (ADR-0038) that feed the Learning Engine.

## Consequences

- Positive: the primary safety property of the ECL — no autonomous high-stakes action at low
  confidence; humans are inserted exactly where judgment or accountability is required.
- Positive: composes with policy enforcement (ADR-0041) — required approvals from CANON-001 §7
  are hard gates independent of confidence.
- Negative: humans become a throughput bottleneck; over-eager escalation causes escalation
  fatigue and erodes trust in the signal. Thresholds must be tuned so escalations are rare and
  meaningful.
- Negative: requires an escalation surface and available reviewers; an unstaffed escalation path
  stalls tasks.
- Neutral: as experience accumulates and confidence rises for a task class, autonomy can safely
  widen — the escalation rate for that class is expected to fall over time, a measurable sign of
  the flywheel working.
