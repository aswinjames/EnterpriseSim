# ADR-0020: Provenance Is Mandatory

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-03-12
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-01, ARCH-02, ARCH-03, ADR-0016, ADR-0021, ADR-0023

## Context

The ECL's value rests on being *inspectable*: a benchmark reviewer, a reflecting Learning
Engine, or a human on escalation must be able to answer "where did this come from?" for any
piece of information a Worker used or produced. A context item with no source, a knowledge
fact with no lineage, or an experience with no origin cannot be trusted, audited, or diagnosed.
"Unauditable behavior" and "provenance loss" are named system-level failure modes (`ARCH-07`):
when a task fails, the single most common productive question is "what was the Worker actually
given, and from where?" — and it is unanswerable without provenance.

We must decide whether provenance is optional metadata or a hard requirement.

## Decision

We will make **provenance mandatory** across the durable and ephemeral stores. No item enters a
context object (`CTX-###`) without a citable source and an inclusion rationale; every knowledge
object (`KN-###`) is sourced and versioned; every experience (`EXP-###`) links to its origin
execution, evaluation and reflection. The Context Layer additionally records *what was dropped
and why* — the dropped-candidate log — because the fix is so often in a doc that was available
but excluded. Provenance references use canonical IDs (`ADR-0022`) so they resolve
(`ADR-0023`), and provenance is immutable once recorded.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** Every reasoning input is traceable, which makes reflection's root-cause
  diagnosis (`ADR-0035` in the full catalog) possible: "the decisive fact was in `KN-047`,
  dropped for budget" is a concrete, actionable finding rather than a guess.
- **Positive.** Provenance is the substrate that makes evaluation's "no score without evidence"
  (`ADR-0016`) and DI's decision traces (`ADR-0021`) coherent — one auditability discipline
  applied everywhere.
- **Negative.** Every retrieval, inclusion and authoring step must capture and persist source
  metadata, adding storage and instrumentation overhead to hot paths.
- **Neutral.** Provenance is recorded even when a task succeeds and nobody ever inspects it;
  the cost is paid uniformly so that the record exists on the rare occasion it is decisive.
