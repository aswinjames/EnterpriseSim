# ADR-0015: Objective-First Evaluation

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-23
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: CANON-001 §4, §8, ARCH-04, ADR-0016, ADR-0017, ADR-0018

## Context

The Evaluation Layer (`ARCH-04`) is what makes EnterpriseSim a benchmark rather than a demo:
it scores what a Worker did against its plan and MCG standards. Evaluation can lean on
*objective* signals (tests passed, CI gates held, coverage met, dependency direction
respected, rollback plan present) or on *subjective/model-assisted* judgments ("is this
well-designed?"). If subjective judgment leads, scores become inconsistent, non-reproducible,
and vulnerable to a model-judge's bias or hallucination — and the benchmark loses its meaning.

We must decide the ordering and weighting of objective versus subjective evaluation.

## Decision

We will make evaluation **objective-first**. Deterministic gates are collected and weighted
*before* any model-assisted judgment, and they dominate the score: unit/contract tests
(`TR-####`), coverage thresholds, security scans, performance budgets, and explicit standards
checks (dependency-direction, rollback plan, API rules, PCI boundary — all from `CANON-001`).
Subjective, model-assisted rubric items are applied last, carry lower weight, and are always
evidence-linked (`ADR-0016`) and confidence-separated (`ADR-0017`). An objective gate failure
can produce a `fail` verdict even when subjective items look good — as when green tests
accompany a forbidden reverse dependency.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** Scores are largely reproducible and hard to argue with, because most of the
  score rests on deterministic, re-runnable gates. This is the precondition for benchmarking
  Workers against each other and over time (`ADR-0048`).
- **Positive.** Canon standards are evaluated *explicitly*, not assumed, so a Worker cannot
  pass by satisfying tests while violating an architectural invariant.
- **Negative.** Genuinely important qualities that resist objective measurement (design
  elegance, maintainability) are under-weighted relative to their real importance; they are
  captured only as lower-confidence subjective items. This is an accepted trade for
  reproducibility.
- **Neutral.** Building rich objective gates (mutation tests, contract tests, standards checks)
  is upfront investment; it pays off as the reusable, model-agnostic core of the benchmark.
