# ADR-0003: Learning Lives Outside the Model (No Fine-Tuning)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-01-12
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: CANON-001 §9 (Stage 8), ARCH-05, ADR-0002, ADR-0005, ADR-0036

## Context

CANON-001 §9 defines a continuous-improvement flywheel: every task should leave the
organization measurably better at the next one. The obvious industry reflex for "make the
model better" is to fine-tune it — fold accumulated task outcomes back into the weights. We
must decide whether MCG's Workers improve by retraining the model or by some other mechanism.

Fine-tuning has serious costs for an enterprise benchmark: it is provider-specific (breaking
model-agnosticism, `ADR-0010`), opaque (you cannot inspect *what* a weight update learned),
irreversible in practice (you cannot cleanly un-learn a bad lesson), prone to catastrophic
forgetting, and slow to close the loop. Worse, it traps the organization's hard-won
competence inside one vendor's weights — the opposite of what a portable, auditable
architecture needs.

## Decision

We will make **all learning live outside the model**. Improvement is realized as *data* —
reflections (`REF-###`), experiences (`EXP-###`), promoted knowledge (`KN-###`), and updated
retrieval/planning policies — produced by the Learning Engine (`ARCH-05`), never as updates
to model weights. A better Worker tomorrow is the *same stateless model* (`ADR-0002`) given a
better Context assembled from a richer Knowledge and Experience base.

Fine-tuning is explicitly out of scope for the ECL's improvement loop. (A provider may ship a
better base model; that is a routing decision at the Gateway, `ADR-0009`, not a mechanism of
enterprise learning.)

## Consequences

- **Positive.** Learning is auditable (every lesson has lineage), reversible (down-weight or
  deprecate the data, `ADR-0047`), and *provider-portable* — change providers on Monday and
  every lesson MCG ever learned still applies on Tuesday. This is the deepest form of vendor
  independence.
- **Positive.** No catastrophic forgetting: additive, append-only experience (`ADR-0005`)
  cannot silently erase prior competence the way a weight update can.
- **Negative.** Improvement is bounded by the quality of retrieval and context assembly, not
  by the model internalizing patterns — a genuinely recurring pattern must be *retrieved* to
  help, so the retrieval and promotion machinery (`ADR-0019`, `ADR-0036`) carries real weight.
- **Neutral.** The ceiling on raw reasoning ability still comes from the chosen model; the ECL
  raises the *effective* ceiling by feeding that reasoning better inputs.
