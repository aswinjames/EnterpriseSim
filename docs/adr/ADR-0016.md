# ADR-0016: No Score Without Evidence

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-26
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-04, ADR-0015, ADR-0020, ADR-0017

## Context

An evaluation score is only useful if it can be *trusted and audited*. A score line that says
"coverage: pass" or "design: partial" with nothing behind it is unfalsifiable — you cannot tell
whether it reflects reality, a model-judge's guess, or a fabrication. Unbacked scores are also
the entry point for two failure modes: judge hallucination (a model-assisted item invents a
justification) and metric-gaming (a Worker optimizes a number nobody checks against an
artifact). For a benchmark, an unauditable score is worse than no score.

We must decide whether scores may exist without linked evidence.

## Decision

We will require that **every score line links to the concrete artifact that justifies it** —
no score without evidence. Each objective gate cites its test run (`TR-####`), scan ID, or the
specific check it ran; each subjective rubric item cites the diff, reviewer note, or named test
that supports it. An evaluation line item that cannot point to evidence is not scored as
`pass`; **missing evidence lowers judgment confidence** (`ADR-0017`) and may render the item
provisional and route it to human review. Evidence links use canonical IDs (`ADR-0022`,
`ADR-0023`), preserving referential integrity.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** Every `EVAL-###` is auditable end to end: a reviewer can follow each score to
  the artifact that earned it, which is what lets EnterpriseSim defend a benchmark result.
- **Positive.** It structurally deters gaming and hallucination — a Worker cannot inflate a
  score without producing the artifact, and a model-judge cannot assert a pass it cannot
  substantiate.
- **Negative.** Evaluation is heavier: gates and rubric items must be instrumented to capture
  and attach evidence, and evidence must be retained (in the object store) for the life of the
  benchmark record. Some qualitative items yield only weak evidence (reviewer notes), which
  correctly caps their confidence.
- **Neutral.** This decision is the evaluation-time counterpart of mandatory provenance
  (`ADR-0020`) in context and knowledge — the same "everything is sourced" principle applied to
  judgment.
