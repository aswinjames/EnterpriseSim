# ADR-0039: Reasoning-Cost Budgets Are First-Class

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-04-21
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-06, ADR-0008, ADR-0009, ADR-0038, RFC-0029

## Context

A Worker's reasoning is not free: every loop iteration is model calls and tokens, and the ECL
loop can iterate (re-retrieve, replan, retry). ARCH-06 names two failure modes — **analysis
paralysis** (endless re-retrieval/replanning that never acts) and **poor routing** (an
over-powered, expensive model for trivial work) — and ARCH-07 names **runaway cost** from
unbounded reasoning loops as a system-level failure. Left implicit, reasoning cost becomes an
unbounded, invisible liability, and a benchmark that ignores it rewards Workers that "think"
expensively without regard to efficiency.

ARCH-06's future-evolution note is explicit: plan the *reasoning itself* — how many
calls/tokens a task merits — as an explicit optimization.

## Decision

We will treat **reasoning-cost budgets as first-class**, per RFC-0029. Every task carries an
explicit budget — an iteration cap and a reasoning-cost allowance (model calls + tokens) — that
Decision Intelligence (ADR-0008) owns and enforces:

- DI sizes the budget to task difficulty and risk tier, and routes models by
  capability/cost/latency through the single Gateway (ADR-0009) — cheap models for trivial
  steps, high-reasoning models only where the difficulty warrants.
- DI detects diminishing returns and stops or escalates (ADR-0040) rather than looping to the
  budget ceiling; hitting the cap is a bounded, explicit event, not silent overspend.
- Reasoning cost (calls + tokens) is an end-to-end signal on the Signals Bus (ADR-0038) and a
  scored dimension in benchmarking (ADR-0048) — quality is always reported against cost and
  latency.

## Consequences

- Positive: bounded, predictable cost; no runaway loops; benchmarks compare Workers and
  providers on a quality/cost/latency frontier, not quality alone — the fair comparison
  EnterpriseSim exists to enable (ARCH-07).
- Positive: cheap-model-first routing lowers the cost floor and pairs with the local SLM
  fallback (ADR-0044).
- Negative: budgets must be calibrated — too tight and a genuinely hard task is cut off before
  it succeeds (a false stop); too loose and the control is toothless. Calibration is itself
  measured and tuned by the Learning Engine.
- Negative: cost accounting depends on providers reporting token/call usage; the Gateway
  normalizes this, and estimates are used where a provider under-reports.
- Neutral: budget exhaustion resolves to escalation or a clearly-explained partial result, never
  a silent give-up — consistent with confidence-honest control (ADR-0040).
