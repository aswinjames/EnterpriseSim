# ADR-0049: Apache-2.0 License and Synthetic-Only Content

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-30
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: CANON-001 (preamble, §1), ADR-0026, ADR-0043, ADR-0050

## Context

EnterpriseSim is an open-source project whose value depends on being freely and safely reusable
by anyone — vendors, researchers and enterprises benchmarking Workers. Two questions must be
settled unambiguously and permanently: under what license the project is released, and what kind
of content it may contain. CANON-001 is explicit that Meridian Commerce Group is entirely
fictional, that any resemblance to a real organization is coincidental, and that every artifact
must remain consistent with the canon. Contamination with real proprietary data, or copied
material from real companies/repositories/tools, would poison the benchmark's legitimacy and
create legal and ethical liability.

## Decision

We will license EnterpriseSim under **Apache-2.0** and require **synthetic-only, original
content** throughout:

- The repository is released under Apache License 2.0 — permissive reuse with an explicit patent
  grant and clear attribution/notice terms, well suited to broad adoption and to open standards
  (ADR-0026).
- All content — MCG's profile, architecture, Jira issues, PRs, incidents, knowledge, experience,
  evaluations, benchmarks — is original and synthetic. No real companies, repositories, tools,
  proprietary documents or real personal/customer data are copied in; public open-source work is
  inspiration only, never copied.
- Sensitivity classification (ADR-0043) models the discipline a real deployment needs, but in
  EnterpriseSim it protects synthetic data — no real regulated data is ever present.
- Contributions are accepted under the project's inbound license/DCO terms consistent with
  Apache-2.0; a contribution introducing non-synthetic or third-party-owned content is rejected.

## Consequences

- Positive: unambiguous, permissive reuse with patent protection; the benchmark is legally and
  ethically safe to publish, fork and extend; alignment with the open-standards posture
  (ADR-0026).
- Positive: synthetic-only content means results can be shared openly without data-privacy or
  confidentiality constraints — a benchmark anyone can run and publish.
- Negative: contributors must be disciplined — realism is achieved by *original* synthesis, not
  by importing real artifacts, which is harder and requires review vigilance to enforce.
- Negative: Apache-2.0's notice/attribution obligations must be honored by downstream users;
  this is a mild compliance burden on reusers.
- Neutral: this decision is itself canon-level; changing the license or the synthetic-only rule
  would require an ADR under canon supremacy (ADR-0050).
