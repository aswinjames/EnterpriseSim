# ADR-0005: Experience Is Append-Only

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-01-19
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: CANON-001 §9 (Stage 7), ARCH-03, ADR-0003, ADR-0020, ADR-0047

## Context

The Experience Layer (`ARCH-03`) is MCG's ever-growing memory of *what it learned by doing* —
distilled, situation-linked lessons (`EXP-###`) earned from real executions, evaluations and
reflections. Some lessons will later prove wrong, narrow, or superseded. The naive response is
to edit or delete a lesson when newer evidence contradicts it. But an experience is *evidence
of what happened*; deleting it destroys the very learning history that makes reflection
(`ARCH-05`) and audit possible, and it risks silently repeating the mistake that produced the
lesson in the first place.

We must decide how experience changes over time without becoming either an unmaintainable pile
of stale advice or a lossy, rewritten record.

## Decision

We will make the Experience Layer **append-only**. An `EXP-###` is written once and its
supporting evidence set only grows: lessons are *refined by adding corroboration or
contradiction*, never silently erased or overwritten. When newer evidence disagrees with a
lesson, it is **down-weighted, not deleted** (`ADR-0047`), reducing its retrieval relevance
while preserving the full history (a down-weighted lesson may recover if new evidence
supports it). Promotion into Knowledge (`ADR-0036`) also leaves the originating experience in
place for traceability.

Every experience carries mandatory lineage (`ADR-0020`) back to its origin execution,
evaluation and reflection; there are no orphan experiences.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** The organization's learning is fully auditable and reversible — a direct
  consequence of learning living outside the model as data (`ADR-0003`). Nothing that happened
  is unlearned; it is only re-weighted.
- **Positive.** Contradictions become first-class signals (contradiction rate) that drive
  reflection, instead of being resolved by a destructive edit that hides the conflict.
- **Negative.** The corpus grows monotonically and can accumulate near-duplicate, low-value
  lessons ("lesson sprawl"). This is mitigated by value-based ranking, consolidation during
  reflection, and promotion pruning — not by deletion.
- **Neutral.** Append-only plus down-weighting means retrieval quality, not storage economy, is
  the thing to optimize; decay is a *scoring* concern (`ADR-0047`), not a *deletion* concern.
