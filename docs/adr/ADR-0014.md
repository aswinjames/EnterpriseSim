# ADR-0014: Autonomy Is Gated on Fused Confidence

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-19
- Deciders: Architecture Office (TEAM-090)
- Related: ARCH-06, ARCH-07, ADR-0008, ADR-0013, ADR-0040, ADR-0041

## Context

Enterprise AI Workers act on real MCG artifacts — they open PRs against Tier 0 services, touch
PCI and auth code, and comment on live incidents. Full autonomy everywhere is unsafe; requiring
a human on every action defeats the purpose. The question is not *whether* Workers act
autonomously but *when* they are permitted to. If autonomy is gated on nothing, or on a single
layer's local confidence, a Worker will act decisively at precisely the moments it should have
hesitated.

Given composable, honest confidence (`ADR-0013`), we must decide what governs the leap from
reasoning to action.

## Decision

We will gate autonomy on **fused confidence** computed by Decision Intelligence (`ADR-0008`),
combined with hard policy stops:

| Fused confidence | Action |
|---|---|
| High | Proceed autonomously. |
| Medium | Re-retrieve / replan to raise confidence *before* acting. |
| Low | Escalate to a human (`ADR-0040`) or abort with a clear explanation. |
| Policy-blocked | Stop regardless of confidence (e.g., PCI boundary, missing required approval, `ADR-0041`). |

**DI never takes high-stakes autonomous action at low confidence.** Thresholds are
risk-adaptive: they tighten for Tier 0 and PCI changes and relax for low-risk internal work,
per `CANON-001` criticality tiers and §7 approval rules. A policy stop overrides confidence in
both directions — high confidence never buys past a required approval.

## Consequences

- **Positive.** The system is safe by default at the boundary that matters — the moment of
  action — and it channels uncertainty into productive responses (re-retrieve, replan,
  escalate) rather than into either reckless action or blanket human dependence.
- **Positive.** Risk-adaptive thresholds let low-risk work run fully autonomously (high
  throughput) while high-risk work gets human oversight, matching effort to stakes.
- **Negative.** Poor calibration has direct cost: an over-confident layer can push fused
  confidence past a gate it should not, and an under-confident system escalates too often and
  erodes trust. Calibration and escalation rate must be monitored as signals.
- **Neutral.** The gate is where policy (`ADR-0041`) and confidence meet; both are hard, and
  neither can be traded for the other.
